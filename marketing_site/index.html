Travel Chatter is a travel phrasebook you write as you go. Capture a phrase and translate it into any language. Hear it spoken aloud, practice using it the next time you meet someone new and get chatting like a local.

Built for travel, not for lessons

Type a phrase in English and Travel Chatter translates it, or record the translation driectly. Edit it, pick a category, mark it as a favorite, and have it at hand whenever you need it.

One book, many places

Most phrasebooks are full of generic phrases you never really need. Travel Chatter isn't - every phrase you add is one you chose. Add a new language before your next trip and your whole phrasebook comes with you, freshly translated.

Say it right

Every phrase can be spoken aloud with text-to-speech. Tap it again within a few seconds and it repeats more slowly, so you can actually catch the pronunciation before you try it on a local.

No lessons, no streaks

Switch to flash card mode to test yourself in either direction - English to your phrase, or your phrase to English - filtered by category, favorites, or whatever you haven't learnt yet. No daily reminders, no habit to keep alive.

Your order, your categories

Group phrases into your own categories, mark favorites, track what you've learnt, and drag phrases into whatever order makes sense to you.

Yours, not ours

Every phrase is stored on your phone - planes, mountains, dead SIMs, it still opens. Back up your whole phrasebook whenever you like, restore it on a new device, or export any language as a CSV to share with friends.

Your words, your homescreen

View your favorites or not learnt phrases, and listen to the audio, straight from your homescreen - no need to open the app at all.
