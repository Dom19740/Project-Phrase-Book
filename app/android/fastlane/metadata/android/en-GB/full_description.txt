Travel Chatter is a travel phrasebook you write yourself. Capture a phrase whenever you hear it, in any language, and keep it. No lessons, no streaks — a book that only contains what you choose to put in it.

ONE BOOK, EVERY LANGUAGE
Most phrasebooks are full of generic phrases you never really need - Where is the bus stop? How much is a train ticket? Travel Chatter doesn't. Every phrase you add is one you want to learn: Nice to meet you. What is your name? One more beer please, translated into whichever languages you're travelling with. Add a new language before your next trip and your whole list comes with you — same phrases, same categories, freshly translated. One book, many places.

WRITE IT AS YOU GO
Hear a useful phrase from a local, a guidebook, or a friend? Add it on the spot. There's no fixed script to follow and no vocabulary you're forced to learn — every phrase you actually need, and nothing you don't.

BUILT FOR TRAVEL, NOT LESSONS
No streaks, no quizzes, no daily reminders to keep a habit alive. Travel Chatter is a reference you reach for in the moment — at a counter, on a train, in a taxi — not a course you have to finish.

WORKS WHERE YOU ARE
Your phrasebook lives on your device. Once a phrase is translated, you can read it, search it, and hear it spoken with no signal and no roaming — exactly when you need it most.

LISTEN, AT YOUR OWN PACE
Every phrase can be spoken aloud with text-to-speech. Tap a phrase again within a few seconds and it repeats more slowly, so you can actually catch the pronunciation.

STUDY WITH FLASH CARDS
Switch to flash card mode to test yourself in either direction — English to your phrase, or your phrase to English — filtered by category, favourites, or what you haven't learnt yet.

YOUR ORDER, YOUR CATEGORIES
Group phrases into your own categories, mark favourites, track what you've learnt, and drag phrases into whatever order makes sense to you. Nothing here is pre-sorted for you.

YOUR PHRASES STAY YOURS
Back up your whole phrasebook to a file whenever you like, restore it on a new device, or export any language as a CSV to share with friends. Nobody sells a phrasebook with your words in it. So we didn't.
